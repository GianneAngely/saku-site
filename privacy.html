Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Saku is a free, open-source app for macOS, made by Gianne Angely. It puts tools in the MacBook's notch. This policy explains what data Saku uses, where that data goes and how you stay in control. It covers the Saku app and this website.

Short version: Saku has no account, no analytics and no server. Your data stays on your Mac. When a feature you use needs the internet, your Mac talks straight to that service, and nothing goes to the developer.

Google user data (Google Tasks)

Connecting Google Tasks is optional. Without it, the To-do tab is a list on your Mac only.

What Saku accesses

When you sign in with Google in Settings › To-do, Saku asks for one permission: the Google Tasks scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks, which Google describes as "Create, edit, organize, and delete all your tasks". With it, Saku reads and writes your task lists and tasks: their titles, notes, due dates, completion status, order and subtasks. Saku doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts, Gmail, Drive or any other Google data. You sign in on Google's own page in your browser, so Saku never sees your Google password.

How Saku uses it

Only to show your tasks in the notch and keep them in sync both ways: changes you make in Saku are sent to Google Tasks, and changes you make elsewhere, for example on your phone, are brought into Saku. Saku doesn't use Google user data for advertising, doesn't build profiles from it, doesn't use it to train AI models, and doesn't pass it to Saku's own Translate or AI features.

Where it's stored

  • A copy of your task lists and tasks is stored on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Saku/todo.json, so the To-do tab opens offline and changes you make offline are sent later.
  • The sign-in tokens Google gives Saku are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Saku has no server. Your tasks travel only between your Mac and Google.

Sharing

Saku doesn't share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. The developer never receives it.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • Settings › To-do › Sign out asks Google to revoke Saku's access, deletes the tokens from the Keychain and removes the Google lists from the copy on your Mac.
  • You can also remove Saku's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Deleting the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Saku removes everything Saku stored on your Mac. Your tasks on Google stay in your Google account.

Saku's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Calendar and Apple Reminders

Saku doesn't sign in to Google for your calendar. It reads and adds events and reminders through the Calendar and Reminders data on your Mac, which macOS syncs with the accounts you added in System Settings › Internet Accounts. Saku never sees those passwords.

What stays on your Mac

Everything else you keep in Saku stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Saku and the app's preferences: notes, clipboard history and snippets, timers, trackers, the file tray, bookmarks, teleprompter scripts, calculator history, saved colours, launcher items, the weather for the city you picked, and your settings. Passwords copied from password managers are never recorded in the clipboard history. API keys you add for cloud services are stored in the macOS Keychain.

macOS permissions

macOS asks before Saku can use these, and Saku only needs them for the feature named:

  • Calendars and Reminders: to show your events and reminders and to add new ones.
  • Camera: for the mirror. The video never leaves your Mac and is never saved.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for the teleprompter to follow your voice. The audio stays on your Mac.
  • Automation: to read and control what's playing in Spotify, Music or your browser.

When Saku goes online

Saku connects to the internet only for features you use. Each request goes straight from your Mac to the service named here, and that service's own privacy policy applies:

  • Weather: Open-Meteo receives the city name you search for and the location of the city you pick. Saku doesn't use your Mac's location.
  • Album covers: Spotify (the link of the track that's playing) and Apple's iTunes Search (the song and artist name). For music in a browser, Saku shows that site's cover image, for example from YouTube.
  • Web tab: the websites you open, like any browser.
  • Google Tasks: Google's Tasks API, as described above.
  • Translate and AI: only when you choose a cloud service and add your own API key, the text you send goes to that service (Google Cloud Translation, DeepL, Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini). Apple's on-device translation and Apple Intelligence keep the text on your Mac.

What Saku never does

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No server: the developer never receives your data.

This website

This site is a few static pages on GitHub Pages, without cookies, analytics or scripts. GitHub may keep visit logs as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

When Saku's use of data changes, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
