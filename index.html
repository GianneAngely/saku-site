Saku

A free pocket of tools in your MacBook's notch.

Hover the notch and it opens into your music, a file tray, timers, your calendar, a to-do list and more. Made for macOS 26 on a MacBook with a notch.

Download Saku, free and open source

What's inside

Every tool sits one hover away and closes again when you move on.

  • Music
  • File tray & AirDrop
  • Clipboard history
  • Pomodoro & timers
  • Calendar & reminders
  • To-do with Google Tasks
  • Notes
  • Teleprompter
  • Battery & system
  • Weather
  • Translate & AI
  • Mirror

Google Tasks in your notch

The To-do tab keeps your lists, subtasks and due dates. If you connect Google Tasks in Settings › To-do, Saku syncs them both ways with your Google account: a task you add on your phone shows up in the notch, and a task you tick off in the notch is done on your phone too.

For that, Saku asks Google for one permission: to create, edit, organize and delete your tasks. Saku uses it only for this sync. A copy of your tasks stays on your Mac so the tab works offline, the sign-in token stays in the macOS Keychain, and nothing goes to the developer or anyone else. The privacy policy has the details.

Your data stays on your Mac

No account, no tracking

Saku has no sign-up, no analytics and no server. The developer never sees your data.

Stored on your Mac

Notes, clipboard, to-dos and settings live in your Mac's Application Support folder. Keys and tokens live in the Keychain.

Online only when you ask

Weather, album covers, the Web tab, Google Tasks and cloud translate or AI talk straight to the service you chose.
